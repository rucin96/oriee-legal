2. What data we process

  • Profile data: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal — provided during onboarding.
  • Food diary: logged meals, portions, nutritional values, streak.
  • Weight and weight trend (including data imported from Apple Health / Health Connect with your consent).
  • Account: identifier, email address (if you create an account), subscription status.
  • When signing in with Google: first and last name and the profile picture URL that Google passes at authentication. We do not use them in the app — our authentication provider stores them and they disappear together with the account.
  • When signing in with Apple: the email address and the first and last name that Apple passes at authentication (name only on first authorisation). If you choose "Hide My Email", we only receive a relay address in the privaterelay.appleid.com domain — we never learn your real address. We do not use the name in the app; our authentication provider stores it and it disappears together with the account.
  • Content for AI recognition: meal text, voice recording converted to text, photos of meals/labels — processed in order to recognise and calculate the values.
  • Technical data necessary for operation (e.g. server-side error logs).

3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

  • Providing the service (calorie counting, diary, trends) — Art. 6(1)(b).
  • AI meal recognition — performance of the contract (point b).
  • Health data (weight, Health) — based on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)); you can withdraw consent at any time in your system / app settings.
  • Subscription settlement — legal obligation and performance of the contract.

4. Recipients / processors

We use trusted providers only to the extent necessary for the service to work:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, server functions) — EU region.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI model) — processing meal text/photos for recognition.
  • RevenueCat and Apple App Store / Google Play — subscription and payment handling.
  • Open Food Facts — a public product database (ODbL) used for search.

We do not sell your data. We do not use nutrition or health data for advertising.

5. Photos and AI

We send photos of meals/labels for processing in order to recognise them. We do not keep the photos after processing — we do not store them on our servers. We do not use your photos to train models.

6. Shared product database

So that the app works faster and more accurately for everyone, we store in our database:

  • the normalised text of a meal description together with the recognised result (a cache that means the same description does not require another AI request),
  • names and nutritional values of products recognised by AI or read from a label.

These records are not linked to your account or identifier — they cannot be used to establish who entered a given description. We do not store voice recordings or photos in this way.

7. Retention

We keep the data for as long as you have an account / actively use the app. Local data sits on your device; account data in Supabase (EU).
